## Instructions
- Package manager: Bun

## Design Context

### Users
General language learners who need a reliable, useful tool for their daily language learning journey.

### Brand Personality
Efficient, simple, and scholarly. The interface should feel like a straightforward, capable utility that gets the job done without fuss.

### Aesthetic Direction
Neutral and achromatic. The color palette will remain strictly token-based (blacks, whites, grays) without introducing a dominant chromatic accent color. It must completely avoid feeling clunky or complex. Both light and dark modes are equally important and should feel native and polished, with no preference given to either mode.

### Design Principles
- **Utility First:** Prioritize efficiency and simplicity over decorative elements. A scholarly focus means the content (language learning material) should take precedence.
- **Strict Neutrality:** Adhere to the achromatic OKLCH scale. Rely on spacing, typography (Geist Sans), and contrast rather than color to organize information.
- **Equitable Dual Modes:** Constantly test and refine both light and dark themes simultaneously to ensure neither feels like an afterthought.
- **Uncluttered Architecture:** Prevent complex navigation or clunky layouts—maintain a clean, snappy, tool-like experience.
